# Try the Budget page

We are testing the page, not you. Please think aloud. You can explore and it is fine to be unsure. There are no personal finances in this example.

Start at http://127.0.0.1:57300/budget. Please don't read the moderator guide first.

1. What is this page telling you about your budget right now?
2. What do the main amounts mean? Which, if any, would you rely on before making another purchase, and what else would you want to know?
3. Is there anything you would check next? Show where you would go and explain why.
4. Find information about income. What is planned, and what has actually happened?
5. Show how you would change an everyday spending amount. Explain what you expect that change to do. You can stop before saving.

For the next tasks, the moderator may switch to a different fictional example. Please continue without reading any expected answers.

6. Find a goal and explain its progress and any dates. What has happened, and what is being planned? Would reserving more for it do anything to a bank account?
7. Open the starting-draft assistance. What information did it use? Change one amount and explain what changes in the result. What do you expect “Use this draft” to do? Does anything here guarantee an outcome or move money?
8. Was anything unclear, surprising or difficult to find? What would you change first?
